Interview with Richard Stallman

This interview with Richard Stallman, founder of the GNU Project and the Free Software Foundation, was originally conducted by the Iranian Linux magazine Linux Review. The interview was later translated into English and republished internationally by OSNews.

Iran presents a particularly interesting case for free software adoption. As a nation not bound by WTO copyright treaties, proprietary software is widely available at low cost, yet the free software movement has gained significant traction among Iranian developers and users who choose freedom for philosophical rather than economic reasons.

The original Linux Review website is no longer online. This page preserves the complete interview for historical and educational purposes.
